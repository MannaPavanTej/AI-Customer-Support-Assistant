# Nexora Technologies Return Policy

## 14-Day Return Window
Customers can initiate a product return within 14 calendar days from the date of package delivery.

## Return Conditions
To qualify for a full return:
- The device must be in original condition with no cosmetic or physical damages.
- All original accessories, power adapters, ethernet cables, manuals, and retail packaging must be included.
- Serial numbers on the product must match the invoice details.

## Return Pickup & Inspection
- Nexora Technologies provides free doorstep pickup for verified returns.
- Our courier partner will pick up the item within 48 hours of return approval.
- Upon arrival at the warehouse, quality inspection takes 2 business days before refund initiation.
