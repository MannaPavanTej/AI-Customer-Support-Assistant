# Nexora Technologies Hardware Warranty Policy

## 1-Year Limited Warranty
All genuine Nexora Technologies smart routers, mesh nodes, and networking peripherals come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase.

## Coverage
- **Covered**: Internal component defects, firmware bricking caused by official updates, power supply failure, antenna degradation under normal indoor use.
- **Not Covered**: Physical damage, liquid spills, unauthorized disassembly, exposure to extreme voltage surges, or lightning strikes.

## Warranty Claim Process
To claim warranty service:
1. Provide the original invoice or registered serial number.
2. Complete remote hardware diagnostics with our technical agent.
3. Bring the device to any authorized Nexora Technologies Service Hub, or request doorstep courier pickup.
