# Nexora Technologies Refund Policy

## Eligibility for Refund
Customers are eligible for a full refund under the following conditions:
1. The order was cancelled prior to shipment dispatch.
2. A return was approved and the physical item was inspected at the Nexora Technologies logistics center.
3. A duplicate payment was deducted for a single transaction.

## Refund Timelines & Processing
- **Credit / Debit Cards**: Refunds are credited back to the original source card within 5 to 7 business days.
- **UPI & Net Banking**: Transferred within 24 to 48 business hours.
- **Wallet Balances**: Immediate or within 24 hours.

## Duplicate Deduction Procedure
If an amount was deducted more than once during checkout, the excess transaction is automatically reversed by the payment gateway within 48 hours. If funds are not reflected after 3 business days, the support agent must request the customer's Transaction Reference (UTR) number to raise an expedited finance ticket.

## Non-Refundable Items
Digital service activation fees, installation charges, and subscription fees for past billing cycles are non-refundable.
