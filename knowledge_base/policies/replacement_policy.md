# Nexora Technologies Replacement Policy

## 30-Day Defective Replacement
If a Nexora Technologies router or IoT device arrives defective, damaged in transit, or develops a hardware malfunction within 30 days of delivery:
1. The customer is eligible for a brand-new unit replacement free of charge.
2. A Nexora Technologies remote diagnostic session must first be conducted to confirm the hardware defect.

## Replacement Procedure
- The support agent creates an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) replacement ticket.
- A courier executive delivers the replacement unit and collects the defective hardware simultaneously (reverse swap).
- If the model is out of stock, an equivalent or upgraded model is provided at no extra fee.
