# Nexora Technologies Payment & Billing FAQ

## What payment methods are supported?
Nexora Technologies accepts:
- Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex)
- Unified Payments Interface (UPI via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
- Net Banking across all major banks
- No-Cost EMI on select credit cards

## What happens if my money was deducted but the order failed?
If your bank account was debited but the order confirmation was not generated:
1. The payment gateway auto-reconciles failed sessions within 2 hours.
2. Funds are reversed back to your source account within 24 to 48 hours.
3. If not received within 3 days, please provide your 12-digit UPI UTR or bank reference number to Nexora Technologies support.

## Can I change my billing address after placing an order?
Billing addresses cannot be changed once an invoice is generated. If required, cancel the order prior to dispatch and place a new order with the updated address.
