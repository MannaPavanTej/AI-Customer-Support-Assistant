# TechCare Shipping & Delivery FAQ

## How long does delivery take?
- **Metro Cities**: 2 to 3 business days.
- **Rest of Country**: 3 to 5 business days.
- **Express Shipping**: Next-day delivery available in select pin codes for orders placed before 1:00 PM.

## How can I track my shipment?
Once your device is dispatched:
1. An SMS and email containing the tracking URL and AWB consignment number are dispatched immediately.
2. Real-time courier milestones can be viewed in the TechCare App under 'My Orders'.

## What should I do if the delivery is delayed?
If the shipment exceeds the estimated delivery date by more than 48 hours, contact support. Our logistics team will initiate an expedited trace with the courier partner.
