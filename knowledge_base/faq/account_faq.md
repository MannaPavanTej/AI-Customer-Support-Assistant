# TechCare Account & Security FAQ

## How do I reset my TechCare account password?
1. Open the TechCare mobile app or website login page.
2. Click on 'Forgot Password'.
3. Enter your registered email or phone number.
4. Enter the 6-digit OTP received via SMS/Email and create a new secure password.

## How to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
Navigate to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication. Enable SMS verification or link with Google Authenticator.

## How do I delete my registered account?
Account deletion requests can be submitted under Privacy Settings. Permanent deletion takes 14 days during which any ongoing warranties must be settled.
