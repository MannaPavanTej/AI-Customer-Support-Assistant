# TechCare Device Setup & Mobile App Pairing

## Initial Hardware Setup
1. Unbox your TechCare Router and connect the high-gain antennas.
2. Connect the included 12V power adapter to the power port and switch on the wall socket.
3. Connect the primary internet Ethernet cable into the blue WAN port.

## Quick App Pairing Process
1. Download the 'TechCare Connect' App from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
2. Ensure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are turned ON on your smartphone.
3. Scan the QR code printed on the bottom label of your TechCare device.
4. Follow on-screen instructions to assign a Network Name (SSID) and create a strong WPA3 Wi-Fi password.
5. Tap 'Complete Setup' and connect your devices to the new network.
