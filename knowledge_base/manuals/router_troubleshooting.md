# TechCare Router Troubleshooting & LED Diagnostics

## Understanding LED Indicator Lights
- **Solid Green / White**: Normal operation, internet connection active.
- **Blinking Green**: Booting up or firmware update in progress. Do not unplug.
- **Blinking Red / Solid Red**: No internet connection detected from WAN modem, or authentication failed.
- **No Light / Dark**: Power supply failure or disconnected adapter.

## Step-by-Step Fix for Red Light Issue
1. **Power Cycle**:
   - Unplug the power adapter from the electrical outlet.
   - Wait 30 seconds to discharge residual power.
   - Plug back in and wait 90 seconds for initialization.
2. **Inspect WAN Cable**:
   - Ensure the blue Ethernet cable from your fiber optic terminal is firmly clicked into the WAN port.
3. **Hard Factory Reset**:
   - Locate the small 'RESET' pinhole on the rear panel.
   - Insert a paperclip and press the internal button firmly for 10 seconds until all LED lights flash amber.
   - Release the button and allow the router to reboot into factory configuration.
